Extra virgin olive oil

A quality culinary fat that may support the body’s natural meal-ending signals.

Key compoundOleic acid · OEA
Suggested timingWith meals

How it fits

The idea behind the food.

Oleic acid participates in the intestinal formation of OEA, a molecule linked with satiety signaling. The effect depends on the full meal pattern, not an isolated spoonful.

A

How to use it

  1. 01Use it as a finishing dressing over vegetables, legumes, or protein.
  2. 02Start with 1 tablespoon and adjust to your energy needs.
  3. 03Do not treat it as permission to add calories without measure.
B

What to look for

  1. 01Look for ‘extra virgin’ and a recent harvest date.
  2. 02Choose a dark container and store it away from light and heat.
  3. 03Expect a fresh, fruity, slightly peppery aroma.

Blueprint pairing

Lentils + mushrooms + EVOO to finish.

This combination is a practical idea, not a medical dose. Adjust it for your tolerance, preferences, and personal goals.

Important note

Nutritional evidence rarely allows a clinically meaningful hormonal effect to be attributed to a single food. This content is educational and does not replace medical care—especially if you use diabetes or weight-loss medication.
