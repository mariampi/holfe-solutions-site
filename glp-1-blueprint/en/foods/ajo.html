Garlic

Preparation matters: chop, wait, and then cook gently to preserve more of its character.

Key compoundAllicin
Suggested timingMeals

How it fits

The idea behind the food.

When garlic is chopped or crushed, alliinase helps form allicin. Immediate heat reduces that reaction; a short rest before cooking allows the process to begin.

A

How to use it

  1. 01Chop or crush the garlic.
  2. 02Let it rest for about 10 minutes.
  3. 03Then add it to cooking, or use it raw if tolerated.
B

What to look for

  1. 01Choose firm, dry cloves without extensive sprouting.
  2. 02Avoid jars with excess sodium or preservatives.
  3. 03Whole garlic gives you more control over preparation.

Blueprint pairing

Rested garlic + EVOO + mushrooms.

This combination is a practical idea, not a medical dose. Adjust it for your tolerance, preferences, and personal goals.

Important note

Nutritional evidence rarely allows a clinically meaningful hormonal effect to be attributed to a single food. This content is educational and does not replace medical care—especially if you use diabetes or weight-loss medication.
