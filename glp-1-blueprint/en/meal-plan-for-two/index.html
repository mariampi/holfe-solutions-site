The rhythm of the day

Three moments.
Zero complication.

Prepare breakfast the night before, choose a drink for morning or afternoon, and save the soup for a comforting dinner. The quantities make two servings.

01 · Breakfast

Prepare the night before

2 servings

Overnight oats

Base

  • 1 cup steel-cut or rolled oats
  • 2 scoops collagen with monk fruit
  • 2 cups unsweetened coconut beverage

To finish

  • Walnuts, divided for two
  • Pumpkin seeds
  • Fresh berries
  • Cinnamon to taste
  1. 1Mix the oats, collagen, and coconut beverage in a container.
  2. 2Cover and refrigerate overnight.
  3. 3Divide into two portions and add walnuts, seeds, berries, and cinnamon just before serving.
Kitchen note “Coconut milk” may mean a carton beverage or concentrated canned milk. For this ratio, choose unsweetened coconut beverage; if using canned, dilute it to your preference and goals.

Pick-me-up

Choose your drink.

Matcha for a caffeinated lift, or ginger for a simple, aromatic alternative.

OR
Option A · Morning

Matcha latte

  • 1–2 teaspoons organic matcha
  • 2 cups water at 175°F / 80°C
  • 2 servings vanilla collagen
  • A splash of milk per person

Whisk matcha with hot—but not boiling—water. Blend in the collagen, finish with milk, and divide between two cups.

Contains caffeine. Drink it early if it affects your sleep.
Option B · Any time

Ginger tea

  • 2 one-inch pieces fresh ginger
  • 2 cups hot water

Thinly slice the ginger, add it to hot water, and steep for 10 minutes. Serve one cup per person.

Begin with less ginger if you have a sensitive stomach.
03 · Dinner

One pot

2 generous servings

Blueprint lentil soup

Ingredients

  • 1 cup dry lentils
  • 4–6 garlic cloves
  • 2–4 tablespoons EVOO
  • Turmeric to taste

Timing

  • Red: 15–20 minutes
  • Green or brown: 25–30 minutes
  • Plus liquid and seasoning for your preferred soup
  1. 1Chop the garlic and let it rest for 10 minutes before heating.
  2. 2Combine lentils with water or broth and turmeric; cook until tender.
  3. 3Stir in the garlic, finish with EVOO, and divide between two bowls.
Adjust the texture Red lentils make a creamier soup; green or brown lentils hold their shape better. Add water or broth until it reaches the consistency you prefer.

Shop for one day

A list for two.

Check what you already have before heading out.

Collagen

Collagen adds protein but is not a complete protein. The rest of the day should include complete or varied protein sources according to your needs.

Diabetes and medication

If you use insulin or other glucose-lowering medication, review portions of oats, lentils, and drinks with your healthcare professional and monitor your individual response.

Portions

“For two” describes recipe yield, not a calorie prescription. Adjust for appetite, goals, and tolerance.
