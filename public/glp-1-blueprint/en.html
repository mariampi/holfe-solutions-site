The paradigm shift

It is not one magic ingredient.
It is an ecosystem.

Blueprint foods do not compete with medical treatment. They organize a nutritional strategy around four functions that work together.

01↯

Activators

Directly stimulate intestinal cells to support GLP-1 release.

02⬡

Protectors

Help preserve the signal by moderating pathways that break down GLP-1.

03◌

Architects

Nourish the microbiome and support a gut environment favorable to sustained response.

04◐

Modulators

Work with metabolic signals related to satiety, glucose, and energy use.

The dual signal

Less hunger.
More satiety.

The goal is not to “force” a hormone, but to build meals that support satiety signals and reduce spikes that trigger early hunger.

Quality shapes the response

Preparation
matters too.

葉

Choose whole, quality foods

Prioritize authentic EVOO, whole spices and leaves, and minimally processed foods.

°

Respect temperature

Do not overheat oil; let chopped garlic rest about 10 minutes before cooking.

◫

Avoid isolated extracts

The guide focuses on foods—not megadoses or concentrates without professional supervision.

◷

Use the right timing

Oats and matcha in the morning; lentils at dinner; aromatics and EVOO with meals.
